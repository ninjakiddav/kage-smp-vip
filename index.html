01 How to join

You need Minecraft: Java Edition on a real, paid account — the server runs in online mode, so cracked accounts can't get in.

STEP 1

Open Minecraft Java

Launch version 26.3 and go to the main menu.

STEP 2

Hit Multiplayer

Choose Multiplayer, then Add Server.

STEP 3

Paste the address

Server address: kage-smp.aternos.me — name it whatever you like, then save.

STEP 4

Join and play

Double-click Kage SMP in your server list. You start with $100 and 3 homes.

02 Your first hour

There's no hand-holding and no kits — here's how players get going.

  • Teleport out with /rtpDrops you somewhere random, 1,000–10,000 blocks from spawn. That's where you build.
  • Hide your baseThere are no land claims and raiding is allowed. If someone finds it, they can take it — build far out and keep it hidden.
  • Make money by sellingFarm and mine, then sell with /sell or list items on the auction house with /ah. Watch item values with /worth.
  • Set your homesUse /sethome at your base so /home always brings you back. Default players get 3 homes.
  • Elytra is earnedNo shop sells it — the only way to get an elytra is raiding End Cities in the End.
  • Talk while you playVoice chat works if you install the Simple Voice Chat mod.

04 Money & shards

Two currencies run the server. Money buys gear and blocks; shards buy the special stuff.

Money

Your main balance — check it any time with /bal. New players start with $100.

  • Sell farmed and mined goods with /sell
  • List items for other players on the auction house, /ah
  • Buy gear and blocks in /shop
  • Send money to a player with /pay
  • Win a duel: +$100,000 (see below)

Shards

Spend them in the shard shop with /store. Check your balance with /shards.

  • 1 every 4 minutes just for playing
  • 10 every 2 minutes while AFK (/afk)
  • 10 for every player kill
  • 10 daily bonus
Duels pay $100,000

Challenge someone with /duel <player>. The winner gets $100,000 from the server — and the loser loses nothing, so there's no reason not to fight.

05 Commands

Every player command on the server. Type them in chat, exactly as written.

Getting around

  • /spawnTeleport back to spawn.
  • /rtpRandom teleport — lands you 1,000 to 10,000 blocks out.
  • /sethomeSave your current spot as a home. Default players get 3.
  • /homeTeleport to one of your saved homes.
  • /tpa <player>Ask to teleport to another player.
  • /tpahere <player>Ask another player to teleport to you.
  • /enderchestOpen your ender chest from anywhere — 54 slots.

Money & shops

  • /balSee how much money you have.
  • /shopThe server shop — buy items with money.
  • /sellSell the item in your hand for money.
  • /worthCheck what the item in your hand is worth.
  • /ahThe auction house — buy from and sell to other players.
  • /pay <player> <amount>Send money from your balance to another player.
  • /shardsSee your shard balance.
  • /storeThe shard shop — spend shards on special items.

Fight & chill

  • /duel <player>Challenge a player to a duel. Winner gets $100,000; loser loses nothing.
  • /afkGo AFK — and earn 10 shards every 2 minutes while you are.
  • /sbToggle the scoreboard on or off.
  • /vipOpens the Kage SMP Store, where ranks are sold.

No command matches that — try a shorter word.
